Data Security & Safety

Privacy Policy

Learn how your account credentials, profile details, and grievances are stored and protected.

1. Information We Collect

When you register on CampusVoice, we collect essential account details to identify you as an active member of the student body, including: full name, university email, roll number, department, phone number, and a secure password hash.

2. Usage of Grievance Data

The details you submit in a complaint (titles, descriptions, location, categories, attachments) are visible to administrative officers and the respective department staff assigned to address your concerns. This data is used solely to inspect, schedule repairs, assign staff, and update resolution progress.

3. Identity Protection (Anonymous Submissions)

If you check the "Submit Anonymously" option, your name, email, and roll number will be hidden in the resolving department's view. The resolving officer sees the ticket as owned by an "Anonymous Student". Your identity remains completely private at the department level to ensure your safety and protect you against bias or retaliation.

4. Passwords & Cryptography

Your password is never stored in plain text. We use industry-standard hashing algorithms (Bcrypt) to securely hash and salt your password before writing it to our databases. This ensures that your actual password remains completely private and secure.

5. Data Retention & Integrity

Grievance data, timelines, comments, and ratings are retained for educational audit, project reporting, and quality analysis purposes. You can modify your phone number and department in your active profile settings at any time.

6. Sharing & Third-Party Transfers

CampusVoice is a closed-loop institutional DBMS platform. We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal data, logs, or complaint histories to any outside third parties or advertising services under any circumstances.
