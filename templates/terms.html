Grievance Portal Rules

Terms of Service

Please read the terms and rules of using our digital complaint management system.

1. Acceptance of Terms

By creating an account or accessing the Campus Complaint Management System (CampusVoice), you agree to be bound by these Terms of Service. These terms constitute a legally binding agreement between you, as a student, faculty member, or admin officer, and the university administration.

2. Authorized Use & Integrity

This portal is intended solely for filing genuine grievances, feedback, and support inquiries related to campus facilities, operations, academics, and services. You agree to provide only truthful, accurate, and complete information. Filing false, malicious, fraudulent, or deliberately misleading complaints is strictly prohibited and will be escalated to the disciplinary committee.

3. Account Security

You are responsible for safeguarding the credentials of your account. You agree not to disclose your login email or password to any third party. Any actions taken under your account will be considered your responsibility.

4. Respectful Communication

All descriptions, attachments, and comments submitted through this portal must adhere to professional campus guidelines. Obscene, defamatory, abusive, threatening, or harassing language directed at administrators, departments, faculty, or other students will result in immediate suspension of account privileges and appropriate administrative actions.

5. Anonymous Filings

Filing complaints anonymously is permitted for safety and sensitivity. However, anonymous submissions remain subject to system audit logs, and the super administrator maintains administrative tools to resolve disputes, investigate abuse, or take action against malicious behavior.

6. Service Modifications

The university reserves the right to modify, suspend, or update features of the CampusVoice portal at any time to improve efficiency, system security, or align with university guidelines.
