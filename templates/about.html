Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers regarding complaint submission guidelines, timelines, and resolution processes.

Simply head over to the "New Complaint" page from the sidebar menu. Choose the correct category, set the priority level, fill out details, optionally attach supporting evidence (images or PDF up to 5MB), choose if you want to submit anonymously, and submit. The system routes it automatically.

Timelines are set based on severity levels:
  • Critical: Addressed in 6–24 hours (emergencies, security threats).
  • High: Resolved within 24–48 hours (WiFi breakdowns, syllabus clashes).
  • Medium: Addressed within 48–72 hours (academic general, equipment repairs).
  • Low: Addressed in 5–7 working days (general suggestions).

When you check the "Submit Anonymously" option, the system hides your name, email, and roll number from the resolving department staff. Your profile info is only stored at the top level for audit and accountability to prevent spam, while the assigned officer sees you as "Anonymous Student".

Absolutely! When an issue is resolved, you are invited to rate the outcome and submit feedback remarks. If you rate the resolution poorly (e.g. 1 or 2 stars), the system automatically flags the grievance and forwards it directly to the Super Administrator's queue for re-investigation.
