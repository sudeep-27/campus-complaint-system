✨ Key Features

Everything You Need for
Effective Grievance Management

Our platform offers a comprehensive suite of tools for students, staff, and administrators to manage campus complaints efficiently.

Instant Submission

Submit complaints in under 60 seconds with our smart forms. Attach photos, documents and set priority levels instantly.

Real-Time Tracking

Monitor complaint progress with live status updates and detailed timelines. Know exactly where your complaint stands.

Smart Notifications

Receive instant in-app notifications whenever your complaint status changes or new remarks are added.

Dept. Auto-Routing

Complaints are automatically routed to the correct department based on category — no manual sorting needed.

Anonymous Complaints

File complaints anonymously when needed. Your identity stays protected while your issue gets full attention.

Admin Analytics

Powerful dashboards with charts and statistics help administrators identify patterns and resolve systemic issues.
